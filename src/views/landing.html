What it does

Everything your school runs on

Each module writes to the same records. Register a student once and they appear in the class list, the timetable, the fee bills and the report cards.

01

Students and admissions

Enrol students with admission numbers and guardian details, place them in a class, and review or decline join proposals submitted from the sign-in screen.

02

Classes and subjects

Keep the catalogue of classes and streams, the subjects taught in each, and which teacher takes which class and subject.

03

Classwork and exams

Teachers publish classwork, homework, tests and exams with PDFs, images, documents and video attached. Students submit answers and files, and teachers grade them in place.

04

Report cards

Generate a term's cards for a class with weighted subject averages, GPA, position, attendance and remarks, then print them as A4 sheets or roll the class over.

05

Fees and payments

Bill a whole class or one student, collect online through Paystack, issue numbered receipts, and keep every balance up to date.

06

Timetable and calendar

A tabular weekly timetable for each class, plus a month calendar on every dashboard carrying coursework deadlines, announcements, fee due dates and school events.

07

Announcements and messages

Publish a notice for everyone, one role or selected classes. A threaded inbox with attachments connects staff and students, and a bell badge shows what is unread.

08

Staff records

One searchable directory of teaching and finance staff with qualifications, subjects and classes, plus the portal logins issued to them.

Payments

Fees, receipts and balances

Billing lives with the finance office, and the student sees the same bill in their own portal. Payments made online are recorded the moment Paystack confirms them. The school connects its own Paystack keys in Settings, so collected fees settle directly into the school's account.

  • Fee items billed to a whole class or to individual students
  • Paystack checkout inside the student portal
  • Receipts numbered RCP-YYYY-NNNNN, printable at any time
  • Outbound transfers and payment reversals kept in the ledger
  • Student balances and outstanding bills, searchable by class
  • Fee due dates on every dashboard calendar
  • Switchable currency symbol for multi-currency schools
Security

How the portal is protected

School records deserve careful handling. These safeguards are built into every request, not switched on later.

Role-scoped endpoints

Every API route checks the signed-in role before it reads or writes. A request outside that role is refused rather than filtered after the fact.

Server-side sessions

Sessions are stored in the database behind an HTTP-only cookie, with a signed token carrier for browsers that block cookies in an embedded view.

CSRF tokens

Every request that changes data carries a token issued with the session, so another site cannot submit on your behalf.

Strict content policy

The app sends hardened HTTP headers and a Content-Security-Policy that blocks inline scripts entirely.

Rate limiting and lockout

The API and the sign-in endpoint are rate limited, and an account is temporarily locked after repeated failed passwords.

Audit trail and safe uploads

Administrative actions and record edits are written to an audit log, and every upload is stored outside the web root and streamed only to entitled users.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get an account?
Teacher and finance logins are created by the school administrator, who issues the email address and password. Students can submit a join proposal from the sign-in screen; the administrator reviews it and, once approved, the student signs in.
Can I sign in with Google?
Yes, when the administrator has connected Google Sign-In in Settings. Where it is not enabled, sign in with the school email address and password issued to you.
Which devices can I use?
Any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. The dashboards are responsive, the sidebar collapses on narrow screens, and nothing has to be installed.
Can fees be paid online?
Yes. A student opens Fees, pays through Paystack and prints a receipt straight away. The finance office sees the payment and the student's updated balance at the same time.
Who can see my records?
Only the roles that need them. A student's coursework, scores and bills are returned to that student and to the staff responsible for their class, and every other request is refused by the server.
Who built this portal, and can it be customised?
The School Management System is designed and built by The Desrex Studio (Acura Solutions). Deployment, branding and extra modules are handled by their team.

Ready to get started?

Sign in with the email address and password issued by your school. Students without an account can submit a join proposal from the same screen, and schools can talk to the team about a deployment of their own.
